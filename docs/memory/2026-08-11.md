Do not build stump content, keywords, or landing pages — Ross does not want that traffic even though the crew does the work.
- **Blog title suffix aligned to the rest of the site** — was ` | AccuRite Excavation Blog` (27 chars) vs ` | AccuRite Excavation` (22) everywhere else. Fixing the suffix, not the titles, pulled 11 of 15 back under ~60 chars.
- **Trailing-slash duplicate URLs deliberately NOT fixed** — `force = true` caused an infinite redirect loop (`d3a266d`, reverted 2026-04-17). Canonicals already consolidate them.

### Open threads / next steps
- **`.html` duplicate URLs — recommended, not done.** `/services/land-clearing.html` and blog `.html` paths return 200 with byte-identical bodies; `/about.html` correctly 301s. `netlify.toml` covers a historical list only, not the whole site. Canonicals mitigate it.
- **Project brief is wrong about the www redirect.** It claims a single hop; measured `http://www` → `https://www` → `https://non-www` = **2 hops**. The other two entry points are 1 hop. Documentation fix, not a code fix — impact is milliseconds.
- **Service-area count still unpublishable** — Ross confirmed 45+ cities but `locations.json` holds 40 and `/locations/` lets a visitor count them. Nothing wrong on the site today; the number just can't be published until the list backs it up.

### Gotchas surfaced
- **A link audit scoped to `href`/`src` misses `og:image`.** The first crawl reported 0 broken and was wrong: `SEOHead.astro` defaulted to `/images/og/og-default.jpg` while only `og-default.png` existed, so **77 of 79 pages served a dead share image**. It only surfaced when the third audit widened to `content=` attributes, `srcset`, CSS `url()` and JSON-LD. Ross had to ask three times before the audit was actually complete.
