## Gotchas surfaced
- DataForSEO Business Data review endpoints use `task_get/{id}` (NO `/advanced` suffix, unlike SERP endpoints), and the reviews task_post wants `location_name`, not `location_coordinate` (a coordinate yields a rejected task → null id → 404 on task_get). Both documented inline in `pull_review_velocity.py`.
- The Maps endpoint (`serp/google/maps`) and the organic local_pack endpoint return slightly different orderings — local_pack is the one that maps to the visible 3-pack.
- Auto-mode classifier hard-blocks self-modification of permission rules and external writes under the user's identity even with an inferred "yes"; do not thrash retrying — surface once and hand off.
