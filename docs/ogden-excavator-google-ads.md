## Red Flags to Watch For

**🚩 High Spend, No Leads:**
- Check landing page is working (test form, phone number)
- Review search terms - might be irrelevant traffic
- Quality Score below 5 = major issues
- Ad copy not matching search intent

**🚩 Lots of Clicks, No Calls/Forms:**
- Landing page not optimized
- Price shock (are you showing prices that scare people?)
- Weak call-to-action
- Slow page load time
- Not mobile-friendly

**🚩 Low Impressions/Clicks:**
- Bids too low
- Quality Score issues
- Ad copy not compelling
- Search volume lower than expected
- Geographic targeting too narrow

## Pro Tips for Maximum ROI

### 1. Call Tracking is ESSENTIAL
- Use unique phone number for Google Ads
- Track which keywords led to calls
- Record calls for quality/training
- Respond to leads within 5 minutes (80% higher close rate)

### 2. Respond FAST
- First contractor to respond wins 50%+ of jobs
- Set up instant lead notifications to phone
- Have someone available to answer calls 7am-7pm minimum
- Return missed calls within 1 hour

### 3. Mobile Optimization is Critical
- 70%+ of excavation searches happen on mobile
- Click-to-call button must be prominent
- Forms should be simple (name, phone, project type)
- Page must load in under 3 seconds on 4G

### 4. Seasonal Adjustments
- **Spring (March-May):** PEAK season - increase budget 30-50%
- **Summer (June-August):** High activity - maintain budget
- **Fall (Sept-Nov):** Good activity - maintain or slight decrease
- **Winter (Dec-Feb):** Slower in Utah - reduce 20-30% or focus on spring scheduling

### 5. Quality Score Optimization
Each +1 in Quality Score = 10-15% lower CPC
- Match ad copy closely to keywords
- Include keyword in headline
- Use geo-modifiers ("Ogden excavation" not just "excavation")
- Fast landing page speed
- Mobile-friendly site
- Relevant landing page content

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Broad Match Without Negative Keywords** - Wastes 30-50% of budget on irrelevant clicks
✅ Use exact/phrase match for expensive keywords

❌ **Sending All Traffic to Homepage** - Reduces conversion 40-60%
✅ Create service-specific landing pages

❌ **Not Using Ad Extensions** - Missing 20-30% extra clicks
✅ Set up ALL relevant extensions

❌ **Set It and Forget It** - Performance degrades over time
✅ Optimize weekly for first 3 months

❌ **Ignoring Mobile Users** - Missing 70% of potential leads
✅ Design for mobile-first

❌ **Generic Ad Copy** - Lower click rates, higher CPCs
✅ Be specific: "Ogden Foundation Excavation - 15 Years, Licensed"

❌ **No Location in Ads** - Wastes impressions outside service area
✅ Include "Ogden" or "Weber County" in every ad
