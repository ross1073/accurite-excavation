## WEEK 1: FREE DOFOLLOW BACKLINKS (Highest Priority)

These are free and give us a followed backlink — the most valuable type for SEO.

### Day 1: Houzz + Porch.com

**Listing 1: Houzz**
1. Go to https://www.houzz.com/pro/sign-up
2. Click "Join as a Professional"
3. Select category: "Excavation Contractor" or "General Contractor"
4. Fill in all business info from the card above
5. Upload 5-10 project photos
6. Add the website URL: https://accuriteexcavation.com
7. Write a brief company bio (use the short description above)
8. Complete verification (usually email)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Backlink:** Yes, dofollow

**Listing 2: Porch.com**
1. Go to https://porch.com/pro
2. Click "Sign Up" or "Claim Your Profile"
3. Search for "AccuRite Excavation" first — if found, claim it
4. If not found, create new listing
5. Fill in all business info from the card above
6. Add photos and description
7. Complete verification
- **Cost:** Free (they'll try to sell paid leads — decline)
- **Backlink:** Yes, dofollow

### Day 2: Yellow Pages + ChamberOfCommerce.com

**Listing 3: Yellow Pages (yp.com)**
1. Go to https://www.yp.com
2. Click "Claim Your Listing" at the bottom of the page
3. Search for "AccuRite Excavation Ogden UT"
4. If found → claim it. If not → add new listing
5. Fill in all business info
6. Select categories: Excavation Contractors, Grading Contractors
7. Add photos and hours
8. Verify by phone or postcard
- **Cost:** Free (they'll try to sell ads — decline)
- **Backlink:** Yes, dofollow

**Listing 4: ChamberOfCommerce.com**
1. Go to https://www.chamberofcommerce.com
2. Click "Add Your Business" (usually in footer)
3. Fill in all business info from the card above
4. Select industry: Construction / Excavation
5. Add description and website URL
6. Verify by email
- **Cost:** Free
- **Backlink:** Yes, dofollow
- **Note:** This is NOT the Ogden Weber Chamber — it's a separate national directory

### Day 3: Apple Business Connect + Bing Places

**Listing 5: Apple Business Connect**
1. Go to https://business.apple.com
2. Sign in with an Apple ID (create one if needed)
3. Search for "AccuRite Excavation"
4. If found → claim it. If not → add new listing
5. Fill in all business info
6. Verify (usually phone call or document upload)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Backlink:** No backlink, but shows up in Apple Maps, Siri, Safari
- **Why it matters:** ~50% of phone users are iPhone — they'll see you in Maps

**Listing 6: Bing Places**
1. Go to https://www.bingplaces.com
2. Sign in with a Microsoft account (create one if needed)
3. Choose "Import from Google Business Profile" — this copies everything automatically
4. If import doesn't work, manually add business info
5. Verify by phone or postcard
- **Cost:** Free
- **Backlink:** No (nofollow), but shows in Bing search + Cortana + Edge

## AFTER EACH LISTING

1. Screenshot the completed profile
2. Save the login credentials somewhere safe (password manager or shared doc)
3. Note the verification status — some take days/weeks to verify
4. Check back after 1 week to confirm the listing is live and the website link is showing

## THINGS TO DECLINE

Directories will call and email trying to upsell you. Standard responses:

- "Would you like to upgrade to a premium listing?" → **No thank you, the free listing is fine.**
- "We can connect you with homeowners looking for excavation!" → **Not at this time, thanks.**
- "Your competitors are advertising with us!" → **We're just looking for the free listing right now.**

They will be persistent. It's okay to say no.
