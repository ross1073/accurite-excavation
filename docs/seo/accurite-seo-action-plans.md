## 1. SUPPLIER & PARTNER BACKLINKS

**Goal:** Get links from businesses AccuRite already works with.
**Who does this:** Ross or admin (requires existing relationships)
**Time:** 1-2 hours of emails/calls over a week

### How It Works
Businesses you already work with have websites. If they link to you, Google sees it as a vote of confidence from a relevant industry source. These are the highest-quality backlinks you can get because they're natural and topically relevant.

### Who to Ask

**Equipment Dealers / Rental Companies**
- Whoever AccuRite buys or rents equipment from (CAT dealers, John Deere, local rental yards)
- Ask: "Can you add us to your customer spotlight or contractor directory?"
- Many dealers have a "Contractors We Work With" page

**Concrete / Material Suppliers**
- Whoever supplies concrete, rebar, pipe, etc.
- Ask: "We'd love to be listed as a recommended contractor on your site"

**Subcontractors You Hire**
- Concrete finishers, plumbers, electricians you sub work to
- Ask: "Can we link to each other? We'll add you to our site too"

**General Contractors Who Hire AccuRite**
- Home builders, commercial GCs who sub excavation to AccuRite
- Ask: "Can you list us as a preferred sub on your website?"

**Insurance / Bonding Company**
- Your liability/bonding carrier may have a contractor directory

### Email Template

Subject: Quick website question

Hey [Name],

This is [Your name] from AccuRite Excavation. We've been working together for [X years] and really value the relationship.

Quick ask — do you have a page on your website that lists contractors or partners you work with? We'd love to be included with a link to our site (accuriteexcavation.com). Happy to add a link back to your site on ours too.

Let me know if that's something you can do. Thanks!

[Your name]
AccuRite Excavation & Hauling
(801) 814-6975

### What to Offer in Return
- Add their logo/link to a "Partners" or "Trusted Suppliers" section on accuriteexcavation.com
- Ross to let me know if he wants me to build this page

## 2. LOCAL NEWS & PR BACKLINKS

**Goal:** Get mentioned in local news/community sites with a link back.
**Who does this:** Ross or admin
**Time:** Ongoing, 1-2 efforts per month

### Easy Wins

**Project Spotlights**
- When AccuRite finishes a notable project (big retaining wall, commercial site, government contract), take photos and write a short summary
- Send to: Standard-Examiner (Ogden), Davis County Clipper, Box Elder News Journal
- Subject line: "Ogden excavation company completes [project] in [city]"
- Include 2-3 photos, a quote from Shawn, and the website URL

**Community Involvement**
- Sponsor a little league team, donate to a school, participate in a community cleanup
- Local papers cover this stuff — send them a photo and a paragraph
- Even a $200 sponsorship can earn a backlink worth 10x that in SEO value

**Awards & Recognition**
- Apply for "Best of Utah" or "Best of Ogden" type awards
- Utah Business Magazine, City Weekly, local chamber awards
- These award pages almost always link to winners

### Where to Send Press Releases

| Outlet | Contact Method | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| Standard-Examiner | standard.net — find "Submit News" or community section | Ogden's main paper |
| Davis County Clipper | davisclipper.com | Davis County coverage |
| KSL.com Community | ksl.com — community calendar/news | Statewide reach, high authority |
| Ogden City website | ogdencity.com | If doing city projects, they sometimes feature contractors |

## 3. GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE — WEEKLY POSTING

**Goal:** Keep the GBP active so Google ranks AccuRite higher in the local map pack.
**Who does this:** Admin (15 min/week)
**When:** Every Monday morning

### Why This Matters
Google rewards businesses that actively post on their GBP. An inactive profile signals to Google that the business might not be active. Regular posts = higher map pack visibility.

### How to Post
1. Go to business.google.com
2. Sign in with the account that manages AccuRite's GBP
3. Click "Add Update" (or "Posts" in the left sidebar)
4. Choose post type: "Update" is the most common
5. Add a photo + short text (see templates below)
6. Click "Publish"

### Weekly Post Templates (Rotate These)

**Week 1: Project Photo**
Photo: Recent job site or completed project
Text: "Another [residential/commercial] excavation project completed in [City], UT. [1 sentence about the project]. Need excavation work? Call (801) 814-6975 for a free estimate."

**Week 2: Seasonal Tip**
Photo: Equipment on site or relevant seasonal image
Text: "Spring is the best time for [basement digs / retaining walls / grading]. The ground is thawed and workable before summer heat sets in. Planning a project? Get your free estimate at accuriteexcavation.com"

**Week 3: Service Highlight**
Photo: Related to the service
Text: "Did you know AccuRite handles [service name]? From [brief description] across Weber, Davis, and Salt Lake counties. E100 licensed with 30+ years of experience. Call us: (801) 814-6975"

**Week 4: Review / Trust**
Photo: Team photo or equipment
Text: "Thank you to our customers for 49 five-star reviews on Google! We're proud to be the Wasatch Front's trusted excavation contractor since 1995. See what our customers say: [Google review link]"

### Monthly Calendar

| Week | Post Type | Service to Feature |
|------|-----------|--------------------|
| 1st Monday | Project photo | Whatever was completed recently |
| 2nd Monday | Seasonal tip | Match the season |
| 3rd Monday | Service highlight | Rotate: residential, commercial, retaining walls, utilities, grading, demolition, septic, hauling, ponds |
| 4th Monday | Review/trust | Always |

### Photo Tips
- Use real project photos, not stock
- Horizontal/landscape orientation works best
- Show equipment, crew, or finished results
- No phone numbers or URLs overlaid on photos

## 4. GOOGLE REVIEW ACQUISITION

**Goal:** Go from 49 reviews to 75+ over the next 3-6 months
**Who does this:** Ross + admin
**When:** After every completed job

### The System

**Step 1: Create a Direct Review Link**
1. Go to Google and search "AccuRite Excavation Ogden"
2. Click your business listing
3. Click "Write a review"
4. Copy that URL from your browser — this is your direct review link
5. Alternatively: go to business.google.com → Home → "Get more reviews" → copy the short link

**Step 2: Send After Every Job**
Within 24 hours of completing a project, send the customer this text:

> Hi [Name], this is Shawn from AccuRite Excavation. Thanks for trusting us with your [project type] project! If you have a minute, a Google review would mean a lot to our small business: [review link]

**Step 3: Follow Up Once**
If no review after 3 days, one follow-up:

> Hey [Name], just following up — if you had a good experience with us, a quick Google review really helps other homeowners find us. Here's the link: [review link]. Thanks again!

### Rules
- Never offer money, discounts, or incentives for reviews (violates Google policy)
- Never ask for a "5-star" review — just ask for an honest review
- Don't send to unhappy customers — resolve issues first
- Respond to EVERY review (positive and negative) within 24-48 hours on GBP

### Responding to Reviews

**Positive review response template:**
"Thank you [Name]! It was great working on your [project type] in [City]. We appreciate you trusting AccuRite with your project. — Shawn"

**Negative review response template:**
"[Name], thank you for the feedback. We take every concern seriously. Please call me directly at (801) 814-6975 so we can make this right. — Shawn Durrant, Owner"

### Tracking
Keep a simple spreadsheet:

| Date | Customer | Project | Text Sent? | Review Received? |
|------|----------|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| 3/23 | John Smith | Basement dig, Ogden | Yes | Yes (5 stars) |

Target: Ask every customer. Expect ~30% conversion rate. To get 25 new reviews, you need to ask ~80 customers.
